Video Games

Video games are a popular form of entertainment enjoyed by millions of people around the world. They provide fun, competition, and even learning opportunities.

Many players enjoy video games because they help reduce stress, improve focus, and allow people to connect with friends online.

Why People Play Video Games

  • Entertainment and fun
  • Stress relief
  • Improves problem-solving skills
  • Play with friends who are long distance
  • Escape from reality

Popular Game Genres

  1. Sports
  2. Action
  3. Adventure
  4. Racing
  5. Role-Playing Games (RPGs)
  6. Horror
